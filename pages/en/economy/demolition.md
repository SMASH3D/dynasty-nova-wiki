# Demolishing a building

> **In short**: you can demolish one level of your buildings to free a field. Demolition is free, takes half the build time of that level, refunds no resources and removes the matching points.
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. Demolishing **drops the building one level** and **frees one field**.
2. Demolition **costs nothing and refunds nothing**, neither when it starts nor when it ends.
3. It **removes the points** of the demolished level.
4. It takes **half the build time** of the level removed, with Automaton Factory and Molecular Assembler bonuses.
5. You cannot demolish the **Planetary Base**, the **Lunar base** or a building at **level 0**.
6. You cannot queue an upgrade and a demolition of the same building at the same time.
7. The **Orbital Dock** cannot be demolished while it is producing units.
8. Demolishing the **Ballistic Arsenal** while it is full destroys no missile.

## Worked example

On a planet with Automaton Factory 4, building Mineral Excavator level 14 took **4,204 seconds** (1 h 10 min).
- Demolishing that level 14 takes half: **2,102 seconds** (35 min).
- You get 1 field back, lose 14.6 economy points and recover no resources.

## Common pitfalls

- **No refund**: demolition is only useful to free space, never to get resources back.
- **Your points go down**: a demolition can move you to another protection tier.
