# Combat

**How battles work and what follows.**

A fleet on an Attack mission fights whatever defends the target planet or moon: ships in orbit and defenses. The battle lasts at most 6 rounds, then come the loot, the debris, the moon chance and the repairs.

```mermaid
graph TB
  A[Fleet arrives] --> B[Battle: at most 6 rounds]
  B --> C{Outcome}
  C -->|Attacker wins| D[Plunder: 50% of the stock, within the holds]
  C --> E[Debris field and moon chance]
  C --> F[Defenses rebuilt: 70% per unit]
  C --> G[Defender's Repair Station]
  C --> H[Battle report for each side]
```
