# Research

**Technologies, tech tree and the laboratory network.**

> **In short**: the 17 technologies are researched in an Innovation Center and apply to all your planets. Only one study runs at a time across the whole empire. Its duration depends on the level's metal and crystal and on the effective Innovation Center level, which Stellar Collaboration raises by pooling your Centers.
