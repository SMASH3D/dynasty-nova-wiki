# Dynasty Nova Wiki

> **Welcome** to the player wiki of Dynasty Nova, the space strategy game. Here you will find the game rules, data for every building, technology, ship and defense, and worked examples. Values come from the **Redline** universe; when a setting changes from one universe to another, a "Universe setting" box says so.
{.is-info}

> Ship names are provisional English translations: the game still shows French names (see the [glossary](/en/getting-started/glossary)).
{.is-warning}

## Where to start?

- **New to the game**: [First steps](/en/getting-started/first-steps), then [The tutorial (quests)](/en/getting-started/tutorial-quests).
- **Coming from OGame**: [Coming from OGame?](/en/getting-started/coming-from-ogame) lists everything that changes.
- **Looking for a number**: [Buildings list](/en/economy/buildings), [Technologies list](/en/research/technologies), [Ships list](/en/fleet/ships), [Defenses list](/en/defense/defenses).
- **A quick question**: the [FAQ](/en/getting-started/faq).
- **No pay-to-win**: nothing you can buy gives a gameplay advantage (see [Premium, shop and Stellar Points](/en/misc/premium)).
