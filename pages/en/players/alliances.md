# Alliances

> **In short**: an alliance brings players together under one banner, with a chat channel, a shared treasury, talents and collective missions. Founding one requires the Stellar Diplomacy technology. Its size depends on its founder's Stellar Diplomacy level.
{.is-info}

<img src="/images/illustrations/research/diplomatie-stellaire.jpg" width="180" alt="Stellar Diplomacy">

## Rules

### Founding
1. Founding an alliance requires **Stellar Diplomacy level 1** (so Quantum Computing 4 and Innovation Center 5).
2. An alliance has a **name** and a **tag**.
3. **Capacity** depends on the **founder's** Stellar Diplomacy level: from 10 members at level 1 to 50 at level 8. A capacity once reached never goes down. The **Embassies** talent adds 2 seats per level (3 levels), so **56 members** at most. Capacity rises when the founder in office completes a Stellar Diplomacy level: a new, better-equipped founder only raises it at their next completed level.

### Joining and leaving
4. You can **apply** to as many alliances as you like and receive several **invitations**, which never expire. Joining an alliance cancels all other requests.
5. **Settling-in period**: during their **first 24 hours**, a new member holds a seat but gets none of the alliance's benefits: no talents, no aid, no missions. They can already vote.
6. After **leaving** an alliance or being **excluded**, you must wait **24 hours** before joining or founding another; you can already apply and receive invitations during that wait. A dissolution imposes no wait. Coming back later starts from scratch: seniority and settling-in period included.

### The founder
7. The founder **cannot leave** the alliance: they must first hand the founding seat to another member, present and a member for at least 24 hours, and then become vice-president. This choice is final.
8. **Automatic succession**: when the founder stops logging in, the members are alerted at 7 then 12 days, and the founder **loses the seat at 14 days**. The successor is chosen by rank, then by seniority in that rank; they must be present and a member for at least 24 hours. The planned heir is shown to the members. With no eligible candidate, the founder keeps the seat.

### Running the alliance
9. Each member has a **rank**: Founder, Vice-president, Officer, Member or Recruit. They can also be given **functions**: Diplomat, Strategist, Quartermaster, Recruiter, Coordinator. Ranks and functions grant rights (see "Who can do what").
10. Changing the **name and tag** (together) costs **500 Stellar Points**, the **description** **50** (clearing it is free), paid by the member who makes the change. The motto, colour, crest and banner are free. The former name stays visible in the alliance's public history.
11. **Disbanding** the alliance loses everything for good (treasury, station, talents, prestige, cosmetics). Members are released with no wait, the channel history is erased and ongoing wars end. The name and tag stay reserved for 30 days, for everyone.
12. **Announcement**: the founder and the vice-president can pin a message at the top of the alliance home, visible to members only. They can also keep up to three alliance channel messages at the top of the feed.
13. **Recruitment**: an alliance is **open** (anyone walks straight in), **by application** (a Recruiter or a leader accepts) or **by invitation** (only an invitation opens a seat).
14. **Excluding** a member takes being at least an Officer and outranking them.
15. **No friendly fire**: from the day you join, you can neither attack nor fire missiles at a member of your alliance. Espionage and transport are still possible.

## Worked example

The founder has Stellar Diplomacy level 5 and the alliance has the Embassies talent at level 2:
- Capacity: 30 (Stellar Diplomacy 5) + 2 × 2 (Embassies) = **34 members**.
- If the founder hands the seat to a member with only Stellar Diplomacy 1, the capacity already reached does not go down.

| Run diplomacy | yes | yes | | Diplomat |
| Manage the treasury | yes | yes | | Quartermaster |

An Officer only acts on members ranked below them. Member and Recruit get no right from their rank. Functions add up. Voting is not a right: every member votes.

## Common pitfalls

- **Pick the founder carefully**: alliance size depends on their research, not the members'.
- **The 24 hours matter**: a new member gets neither alliance bonuses nor the right to inherit the founding seat during their first 24 hours.
- **Switching alliances costs time**: 24 hours of waiting, and your seniority starts over.
- **A disbanded alliance cannot be recovered**: everything it built is lost.
