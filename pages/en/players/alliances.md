# Alliances

> **In short**: an alliance brings players together under one banner: a chat channel, a shared treasury, a station, talents, collective missions, pacts and wars. Founding one takes the Stellar Diplomacy research; its size depends on its founder's Stellar Diplomacy level.
{.is-info}

## Rules

### Founding
1. Founding an alliance requires **Stellar Diplomacy level 1** (so Quantum Computing 4 and Innovation Center 5).
2. An alliance has a **name** and a **tag**.
3. **Capacity** depends on the **founder's** Stellar Diplomacy level: from 10 members at level 1 to 50 at level 8. It rises when the founder in office completes a level, and never goes down. The **Embassies** talent adds 2 seats per level (3 levels), so **56 members** at most.

### Joining and leaving
4. **Recruitment**: an alliance is **open** (anyone walks straight in), **by application** (a Recruiter or a leader accepts; this is the default) or **by invitation** (only an invitation opens a seat).
5. You can **apply** to as many alliances as you like and receive several **invitations**; nothing expires. Joining an alliance cancels all other requests.
6. **Settling-in period**: during their **first 24 hours**, a new member holds a seat but gets no talents, no aid and no missions. They can already vote.
7. After **leaving** an alliance or being **excluded**, you must wait **24 hours** before joining or founding another; during that wait you can already apply and receive invitations. A dissolution imposes no wait. Coming back later starts from scratch: seniority and settling-in period included.
8. **Excluding** a member takes being at least an Officer and outranking them.
9. **No friendly fire**: from the day you join, you can neither attack nor fire missiles at a member of your alliance. Espionage and transport are still possible.

### Ranks and functions
10. Each member has a **rank**: Founder, Vice-president, Officer, Member or Recruit. You only act on a member ranked below you.
11. A member can also be given one or more **functions**: Diplomat, Strategist, Quartermaster, Recruiter, Coordinator. Ranks and functions grant rights (see "Who can do what"). Voting is not a right: every member votes.

### The founder
12. The founder **cannot leave** the alliance: they must first hand the founding seat to a member who is present and has been a member for at least 24 hours, and then become vice-president. This choice is final.
13. **Automatic succession**: when the founder stops logging in, the members are alerted at 7 then 12 days, and the founder **loses the seat at 14 days**. The successor is chosen by rank, then by seniority in that rank; they must be present and a member for at least 24 hours. The planned heir is shown to the members. With no eligible candidate, the founder keeps the seat.

### Identity, announcement, dissolution
14. Changing the **name and tag** (together) costs **500 Stellar Points**, the **description** **50** (clearing it is free), paid by the member who makes the change. The motto, colour, crest and banner are free. The former name stays visible in the alliance's public history.
15. **Announcement**: the founder and the vice-president can pin a message at the top of the alliance home, visible to members only, and keep up to three alliance channel messages at the top of the feed.
16. **Dissolving** the alliance loses everything for good: treasury, station, talents, cosmetics. The channel history is erased, ongoing wars end and members are released with no wait. The name and tag stay reserved for 30 days, for everyone. You confirm by typing the name or the tag.

## Worked example

The founder has Stellar Diplomacy level 5 and the alliance has taken the Embassies talent at level 2:
- Capacity: 30 (Stellar Diplomacy 5) + 2 × 2 (Embassies) = **34 members**.
- If the founder hands the seat to a member with only Stellar Diplomacy 1, the capacity reached does not go down.

## Common pitfalls

- **Pick the founder carefully**: the alliance's size depends on their research, not the members'.
- **The 24 hours matter**: a new member has neither the alliance bonuses nor the right to inherit the founding seat during their first 24 hours.
- **Switching alliances costs time**: a 24-hour wait, and your seniority starts again from zero.
- **A dissolved alliance cannot be recovered**: everything it built is lost.
