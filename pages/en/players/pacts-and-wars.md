# Pacts, fair play and wars

> **In short**: alliances sign pacts (non-aggression or alliance) and declare war through a vote of all their members. Neither pacts nor wars change the combat rules: what is at stake is the alliance's word, measured by its fair-play score, and the war's score.
{.is-info}

## Rules

### Pacts
1. Two kinds of pact: **non-aggression** and **alliance**. One pact at a time between two alliances, in the same universe.
2. A pact is offered directly by a member with the "Run diplomacy" right, or through a vote whose adoption sends the offer. It binds nobody until **the other alliance has signed it**; an offer can be refused or withdrawn.
3. **Attacking** a member of an alliance bound by a pact is still possible, but the attack is recorded as a **violation** as soon as the fleet leaves and costs your alliance fair-play points: **10 points** for a non-aggression pact, **15 points** for an alliance pact. Only raids and missile strikes count; espionage and transport do not. The game warns you before sending, without blocking you.
4. Recalling the fleet does not undo the violation. An alliance loses no more than **30 points** per day. A violation by a member still in their settling-in period (their first 24 hours) costs the alliance nothing, but it stays on record.
5. **Denouncing** a pact is public and cannot be undone. The pact still binds for **24 hours** after denunciation: attacking during that notice is still a violation. Breaking a pact this way costs nothing.
6. **Declaring war** on an alliance bound by a pact counts as a violation, at the same price, and ends the pact on the spot.

### Fair play
7. The fair-play score measures what the alliance's word is worth. It runs from **0 to 100** and starts at **100**. It shows as five grades (see Detailed data), on the alliance's public profile.
8. A violation lowers it. Each day the alliance has at least one pact in force and commits no violation earns **1 point**, plus 1 per level of the **Kept word** talent, never above 100.
9. Violations committed and suffered can be looked up. A player's violation count follows them from one alliance to the next.

### Votes
10. **Every member votes**, recruits included: one ballot each, which cannot be changed.
11. Whoever asks the question picks how long the vote lasts, **from 1 to 24 hours**, and can close it early. Only one open question at a time per subject and target.
12. The question is adopted by a **majority of the votes cast**. A tie, or no vote at all, rejects it.
13. Subjects: **declaring war** and **offering a pact** ("Run diplomacy" right), and the **free question** (diplomacy or announcement right), which gathers the members' opinion with no automatic effect.

### Wars
14. A war is only declared through an **adopted vote**; adoption declares it immediately.
15. The war becomes **mutual** once both sides have declared it, the other side voting in turn.
16. It ends with a **peace** offered without a vote by a member with the diplomacy right and accepted by the other side, or with the dissolution of either side. There is no surrender and no automatic end.
17. **Score**: the war counts the battles won by each side, attacking or defending; a draw does not count. The score shows during the war, then stays in the history of past wars. Enemy cells are marked on the map.
18. War **changes nothing** about combat, plunder, the [attack limit](/en/players/attack-limit) or beginner protection.

## Worked example

Your alliance has a non-aggression pact with alliance B and the Kept word talent at level 2.
- Each day of pact kept: 1 + 2 = **+3 fair-play points**.
- One of your members attacks a player of B: **−10 points**, even if they recall their fleet, and no gain that day.
- Four attacks on the same day: 4 × 10 = 40, but the loss is capped at **30 points**.

A war vote in a 20-member alliance: 7 for, 6 against, 7 abstentions. 7 > 6: war is **declared**.

## Common pitfalls

- **The 24-hour notice applies**: denouncing a pact and attacking right away is still a violation.
- **Recalling erases nothing**: the violation is recorded as soon as the attack is launched.
- **Declaring war on a partner costs fair play**: denounce the pact first and wait out the notice.
- **War grants no extra rights**: the limit of 6 attacks on the same player in 24 hours and beginner protection still apply.
