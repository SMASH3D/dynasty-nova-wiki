# Pacts, fair play and wars

> **In short**: alliances can sign pacts (non-aggression or alliance) and declare war on each other by vote. Neither pacts nor wars change combat rules: what is at stake is the alliance's word, measured by its fair-play score.
{.is-info}

## Rules

### Pacts
1. Two kinds of pact: **non-aggression** and **alliance**.
2. A pact binds nobody until **the other alliance has signed it**.
3. **Attacking** a member of an alliance bound by a pact is still possible, but the attack is recorded as a **violation** and costs your alliance fair-play points: **10 points** for a non-aggression pact, **15 points** for an alliance pact. Recalling the fleet does not undo the violation. Only raids and missile strikes count; espionage and transport do not. An alliance loses no more than **30** fair-play points per day. A violation by a member still in their [settling-in period](/en/players/alliances) (their first 24 hours) costs the alliance nothing, but it stays on record.
4. **Denouncing** a pact is public and cannot be undone. The pact still binds for **24 hours** after denunciation: attacking during that notice is still a violation.
5. Breaking a pact cleanly costs nothing, but takes those 24 hours of notice. **Declaring war** on an alliance bound by a pact counts as a violation, at the same price, and ends the pact on the spot.

### Fair play
6. The fair-play score measures what the alliance's word is worth. It runs from 0 to 100 and starts at 100. It shows as five grades: **Exemplary** (90 and up), **Reliable** (70 and up), **Questionable** (40 and up), **Unreliable** (15 and up), **Forsworn** (under 15).
7. A violation lowers it. Each day the alliance has at least one pact in force and commits no violation earns **1 point**, plus 1 per level of the **Kept word** talent, never above 100.

### Wars
8. A war is decided by a **vote**. Opening the question takes the "Run diplomacy" right; **every member votes**, recruits included, one ballot each, which cannot be changed.
9. The question is adopted by a **majority of the votes cast**. A tie, or no vote at all, rejects it. Adoption declares war immediately. Whoever asks the question picks how long the vote lasts, **from 1 to 24 hours**, and can close it early.
10. The war becomes **mutual** once both sides have declared it. It ends with a **peace** offered without a vote by a member with the diplomacy right and accepted by the other side, or with the dissolution of either side.
11. War counts the battles won by each side, attacking or defending (a draw does not count), and marks enemy cells on the map. The score shows during the war, then stays in the history of past wars. It **changes nothing** about combat, plunder or the [attack limit](/en/players/attack-limit).

### Other votes
12. A **pact** can be offered directly by a member with the diplomacy right, or put to a vote: its adoption sends the offer.
13. A **free question** (diplomacy or announcement right) gathers the members' opinion, with no automatic effect.

## Worked example

Your alliance has a non-aggression pact with alliance B and the Kept word talent at level 2.
- Each day the pact is kept: 1 + 2 = **+3 fair-play points**.
- One of your members attacks a player from B: **−10 points**, even if they recall the fleet.

## Common pitfalls

- **The 24-hour notice applies**: denouncing a pact and attacking right away is still a violation.
- **Recalling erases nothing**: the violation is recorded as soon as the attack is launched.
- **War grants no extra rights**: the limit of 6 attacks on the same player in 24 hours and beginner protection still apply.
