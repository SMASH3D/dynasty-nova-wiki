# Deleting your account

> **In short**: you can delete your account yourself from the Settings. The deletion takes place **7 days after the request**. During those 7 days, your account is protected in all your universes and you can still change your mind. At the end, you leave all your universes, your planets are abandoned and the deletion is final.
{.is-info}

## Rules

### Asking for the deletion
1. The request is made in the **Settings**, **Account** tab, "Delete my account" block. A window first sums up what the deletion takes: your planets and moons per universe, your stellar points, your premium, the private universes you created.
2. To confirm, you **type your username** and your **password**.
3. The deletion is scheduled **7 days later**, to the minute. An email confirms it, with a link to cancel.
4. The day before, a **reminder email** is sent with a new cancel link. The link in the first email no longer works.

### During the 7 days: the protection
5. From the request, your account goes **into vacation mode in all your universes**, even with a fleet in flight, an order under way or an attack on its way. A universe where you were already on vacation stays as it was.
6. As in [vacation mode](/en/players/vacation-mode): you can no longer act, other players can no longer target you, and **production stops**. Fleets and missiles **already sent** against you still arrive.
7. A banner shows the deletion date on every screen, with a "Cancel the deletion" button.

### Your alliances
8. If you **founded an alliance**, the founder role passes **at the request** to another member: the one you pick in the window, among the members who could take the seat, or failing that the one the automatic succession picks (the best claim, otherwise the most senior member).
9. If you are **alone** in your alliance, you keep it to the end: it is dissolved with your account.

### Cancelling
10. You can cancel during the 7 days: from the banner or the Settings, or with the **email's link**, without signing in. Opening the link cancels nothing: you have to click the button on the page.
11. After a cancellation, the protection comes down **48 hours after the request at the earliest**: at once if that time has passed, at the 48th hour otherwise.
12. A new request is only possible **30 days after the cancellation**.
13. The founder role handed over at the request **does not come back to you**.

### On the day of the deletion
14. You leave **all your universes**: your fleets in flight are lost, your orders under way are cancelled and refunded on the planet, and your planets and moons are **abandoned** under each universe's conditions. A missile already fired still lands.
15. Your **stellar points**, your **cosmetic items** and your Stellar Bank **Credits** are lost.
16. Your **private messages** are erased, along with your reactions. Your **public messages** stay, signed "Deleted player".
17. The public battle reports you had shared disappear; those showing you as the opponent no longer show your name.
18. Your **username** only becomes available again after **30 days**.
19. A last email confirms the deletion. It is **final**: the account cannot be reopened.

### Subscriptions
20. The request **stops the renewal** of your premium and of the subscriptions of the private universes you created. Nothing already paid is refunded.
21. A private universe you created **stays open** until the end of the period paid, then ends as usual.
22. If you cancel the deletion while the period paid is still running, the renewal resumes.

## Worked example

You ask for the deletion on 16 October at 10:00:
- the deletion is scheduled on **23 October at 10:00**;
- the reminder email is sent from **22 October at 10:00**;
- you cancel on 17 October at 08:00: the protection comes down on **18 October at 10:00** (48 hours after the request), and you cannot ask for the deletion again before **16 November at 08:00**.

## Common pitfalls

- **The protection does not stop attacks on their way**: a fleet sent against you before the request still arrives.
- **Coming back from vacation lifts the protection**: after 48 hours, the vacation mode's "Come back" button still works. Using it exposes you again in that universe, and the deletion goes ahead.
- **The founder role goes at once**: pick your successor before confirming, it will not be given back if you cancel.
- **Paid premium is not refunded**, even with several months left.
