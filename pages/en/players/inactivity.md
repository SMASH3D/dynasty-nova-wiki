# Inactivity

> **In short**: a player who does not show up in a universe for longer than the inactivity delay (7 days on Redline) loses their point protection and becomes attackable by anyone. There is no other stage: no account deletion, no automatic abandonment of planets.
{.is-info}

> **Universe setting**: the inactivity delay may differ from one universe to another; 0 turns inactivity off.
> Default: **14 days** · Redline: **7 days**
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. You become **inactive** in a universe when your last activity there is **more than 7 days** old on Redline (14 by default).
2. **Any visit counts as activity**: opening a screen, switching planet or looking at the galaxy is enough. Activity is recorded for the universe you are playing in at that moment: playing in another universe does not count for this one.
3. **Effect**: an inactive player loses their [point protection](/en/players/beginner-protection). Anyone may attack them, whatever the gap, including a much smaller player.
4. Only the **defender's** inactivity counts. As soon as you come back, you are active and protected again.
5. That is the **only effect** on your empire: the account is not deleted and planets are not abandoned.
6. [Newcomer protection](/en/players/beginner-protection) does not depend on activity: it lasts 7 days, whether you play or not.
7. In [vacation mode](/en/players/vacation-mode), nobody can attack you, inactive or not. On your return, your activity is recorded again.
8. **Alliance founder**: an absent founder is flagged to the alliance after **7 days**, then **12 days**, and **loses their seat at 14 days** to a successor. These delays are the same in every universe, and vacation days count (see [Alliances](/en/players/alliances)).
9. Non-human (PvE) players are never inactive.

## Worked example

On Redline, you have 300 points and leave for three weeks without turning on vacation mode. Your last visit was on a Monday at 20:00.
- Until the following Monday 20:00, a 50,000-point player cannot attack you: they have more than 3 times your points (1:3 tier).
- After the following Monday 20:00 (7 days), you are inactive: that player may attack you.
- You still cannot attack them: they are active, and the gap protects them.
- If you founded an alliance, the alliance is warned on day 7 and then day 12, and your seat goes to another member on day 14.

## Common pitfalls

- **Plan a long absence**: turn on vacation mode, otherwise you lose your protection after 7 days on Redline.
- **Playing elsewhere is not enough**: activity is counted universe by universe.
- **Alliance founders, hand over**: after 14 days, even on vacation, your seat goes to another member.
