# Pseudo, email and password

> **In short**: you change your **pseudo**, your **email address** and your **password** yourself, in the Settings, **Account** tab. Each change asks for your **current password**. The pseudo is free, once every **7 days**, and it is also your login. A new address is confirmed with a **6-digit code**, valid for **30 minutes**, once every **24 hours**. A new password closes your **other sessions**.
{.is-info}

## Rules

### For every change
1. Everything happens in the **Settings**, **Account** tab, "Credentials" block: a "Change" button next to the pseudo, the email address and the password.
2. Each change asks for your **current password**. A session left open on another device is therefore not enough to take your account.
3. After too many attempts, you have to wait a few minutes before trying again.
4. **During the 7 days of an [account deletion](/en/players/account-deletion)**, nothing can be changed: neither pseudo, nor address, nor password. Cancel the deletion first.

### The pseudo
5. Changing your pseudo is **free**, at most **once every 7 days**. The first change is possible at any time; after that, the Account tab shows the date of the next one.
6. The new pseudo follows the registration rules: **5 to 50 characters**, no space. It must be neither taken by another account nor reserved.
7. Your pseudo is also your **login**: from the change on, you sign in with the new one, the former one no longer works. An email reminds you of the new pseudo.
8. For **30 days**, your profile shows "Formerly" followed by your former pseudo, and **nobody else can take it**. You can take it back yourself during those 30 days, once 7 days have passed since your last change.
9. Reports already written keep the former pseudo. During those 30 days, clicking it **still opens your profile**, under your new pseudo.
10. Changing only upper and lower case (for example `vega` to `Vega`) counts as a change for the 7-day delay, but nothing is reserved or shown as a former pseudo.
11. Your sessions stay open: changing your pseudo signs no device out.

### The email address
12. You enter the new address and your current password: a **6-digit code** is sent to the **new address**. It stays valid for **30 minutes**.
13. Until the code is entered, your **current address remains the account's**, for every email, including the forgotten password ones. The Account tab reminds you of the pending address and the code's deadline, and offers "Enter the code".
14. The code did not arrive? "Nothing arrived: send a new code" sends a new code, which replaces the previous one. An expired code no longer works: start again.
15. Once the code is entered, the new address becomes the account's, and the **former address receives a last email** warning it of the change and giving the new one.
16. You have to wait **24 hours** between two changes of address. The Account tab shows the date of the next one.
17. An address already used by another account cannot be taken.

### The password
18. You enter the current password, then the new one twice: **at least 5 characters**, different from the current one.
19. For your safety, **all your other sessions are closed**: on your other devices and browsers, you have to **sign in again** with the new password. The device that made the change stays signed in.
20. An email confirms the change. It is also sent after a reset through "Forgot password?".

## Worked example

On October 27 at 14:00, you go from `Rigel` to `Vega`:
- from now on you sign in with `Vega`;
- next change of pseudo possible on **November 3 at 14:00**;
- until **November 26 at 14:00**, your profile shows "Formerly Rigel", nobody else can take `Rigel`, and an old report naming `Rigel` leads to your profile.

The same day at 14:05, you ask for a new address:
- the code sent to the new address is valid until **14:35**;
- you enter it at 14:12: the new address becomes the account's, the former one receives the warning email;
- next change of address possible on **October 28 at 14:12**.

## Common pitfalls

- **Write down your new pseudo**: it is the one to type to sign in. The confirmation email reminds you of it.
- **The code goes to the new address**, not the current one: check that it is spelled right and look in your spam folder.
- **Changing your password signs your other devices out**, phone included: plan to sign in there again.
- **A change of case alone also starts the 7-day delay.**
- **An email you did not expect** (pseudo, address or password changed): change your password, or use "Forgot password?" if you can no longer sign in, then write to the support.
