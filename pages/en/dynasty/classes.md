# The classes

> **In short**: the class is the way you play. It carries a talent tree made of mechanics nobody else has. Six classes are open to every dynasty, four are reserved to a single one. You can change class, within your dynasty, once a week.
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. You choose your class **among your dynasty's classes**: the six common classes and the two exclusive to your dynasty.
2. The class carries the **class tree**: 3 branches of 9 rows. See [Talents](/en/dynasty/talents).
3. Each mechanic has **a single owning class**: fleet combat belongs to the Admiral, defences and missiles to the Veteran, salvage outside combat to the Archivist, energy shortage to the Energetician, tracking spies to the Shadow.
4. The generic bonuses (production, research, fleet speed…) are in the [common tree](/en/dynasty/common-tree), the same for every class.

## Class mastery

5. **Mastery** measures your experience in the class you play. It gives the class tree's points: **1 point per 2 mastery levels**, rounded up, **25 at most**.
6. A class played since you joined the universe has its mastery **at your character's level**.
7. A class taken along the way starts from its own mastery and climbs with the experience you earn afterwards, on the same curve as your level: catching up takes the same effort as levelling.
8. Mastery **never exceeds your level**.
9. A class never played stands at 0. It reaches level 1 with your first experience in that class.
10. Each class **keeps its mastery**: coming back to a class you already played resumes where you left it.

## Changing class

11. You can take another class **of your dynasty** for **50 Stellar Points**.
12. A change is possible only **once every 7 days**.
13. The **class tree is emptied**. Your new class points depend on the new class's mastery. Its first reset becomes free again.
14. The **common tree does not move**.
15. The dynasty never changes.

## Worked example

**Changing class at level 30**
- You have played the Builder since the start: mastery 30, so **15 class points**.
- You switch to the Admiral, never played: mastery 0, **0 points**. Your common tree keeps its 30 points.
- Your first experience as an Admiral puts it at mastery 1: **1 point**. It then climbs with the experience you earn, never above your level.
- If you come back to the Builder a week later, it resumes its mastery of 30.

## Common pitfalls

- **Changing class to try it out**: the class tree starts almost from scratch with a new class. Read its page first.
- **Forgetting the delay**: after a change, you must wait 7 days before the next one.
- **Believing the common tree is lost**: it never moves when you change class.
