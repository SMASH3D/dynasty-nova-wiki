# The common tree

> **In short**: the common tree is the same for every dynasty and every class. It gets 1 point per level, 50 at most, and sets your own pace: **Prosperity** to produce and build, **Exploration** to research and expand, **Logistics** for your fleets. 50 points are enough for a full branch and most of a second one.
{.is-info}

> Ship names are provisional English translations: the game still shows French names (see the [glossary](/en/getting-started/glossary)).
{.is-warning}

> **Universe setting**: a player counts as inactive (abandoned target) after a number of days without activity that depends on the universe.
> Default: **14 days** · Redline: **7 days**
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. 3 branches of 10 rows, 28 points per branch, 84 in total. You place **50 at most**.
2. A node only opens once the node above it is full. Rows open at 8, 20 and 32 points placed above, every branch together: a branch alone holds 16 points in its rows 1 to 6 and 22 in its rows 1 to 8, so you need **4 points elsewhere** before its row 7 and **10** before its row 9. The ultimate (row 10) also requires level 40 and the full branch. See [Talents](/en/dynasty/talents).
3. Rows 3 and 6 are **choices** in all three branches: one point, option A or B. Row 8 is a choice in Prosperity and Exploration; in Logistics, it is a key (Priority Loading).
4. The common tree **does not change** when you change class.
5. Bonuses that only serve you (production, research, construction) go high. Those that affect other players (fleet speed, plunder) stay moderate.
6. Fleet speed bonuses **divide the flight duration**: +50% = a flight 1.5 times shorter.

## Prosperity

*What your worlds produce and build.*

| Row | Node | Type | Effect (full node) | Per rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Extraction** | 3 ranks | Your metal, crystal and hydrogen mines produce 9% more. | 3 × 3% |
| 2 | **Automated Cranes** | 5 ranks | Your buildings are built 10% faster. | 5 × 2% |
| 3 | **A · Warehouses** | choice | Your storage holds 20% more resources. | |
|  | **B · Buried Vaults** |  | A raider attacking you carries off 5 pts less of your resources. | |
| 4 | **Rich Veins** | 5 ranks | Your metal, crystal and hydrogen mines produce 15% more. | 5 × 3% |
| 5 | **Plasma Foundry** | key | The effect of your plasma technology on production rises by 50%. |  |
| 6 | **A · Optimised Grid** | choice | Your power plants and satellites produce 8% more energy. | |
|  | **B · Thriving Colonies** |  | Your colonies produce 10% more resources. Your home planet is not included. | |
| 7 | **Workshops** | 5 ranks | Your ships and defences are built 10% faster. | 5 × 2% |
| 8 | **A · Resource Bridge** | choice | Once a day, transfer up to 2 h of production from one of your planets to another, instantly and without a fleet. | |
|  | **B · Safety Stock** |  | When your storage is full, your mines keep producing at 25% instead of stopping. | |
| 9 | **Night Shifts** | 5 ranks | Your buildings are built 10% faster. | 5 × 2% |
| 10 | **Golden Age** | ultimate | Your mines produce 1% more and your buildings are built 5% faster still. |  |

- Production bonuses apply to the raw production of the three mines, before the energy ratio. They add up, then are capped.
- **Warehouses** adds up with the Logistics Center and applies to the planet's whole capacity, Planetary Base included. **Buried Vaults** takes 5 points off the plunder rate you suffer (50% → 45%), never below 30%.
- **Plasma Foundry**: your Plasma Mastery level counts one and a half times for production. This bonus is computed apart from the production cap.
- **Optimised Grid** raises the energy produced by Photovoltaic Sensors, the Thermonuclear Reactor and Solar Collectors. **Thriving Colonies** applies to every planet that is neither your home planet nor a moon (colonies and conquered worlds), past the 45% cap.
- **Resource Bridge**: once per rolling 24 hours for your whole empire, to any other of your planets or moons. The limit is 2 hours of the departure planet's current production, the three resources added together and split as you wish, never more than its stock. The arrival planet may go past its storage.
- **Safety Stock**: when a storage building is full, 25% of what the mine would have produced is still credited, above capacity.

Full branch: mines **+25%**, buildings **+25%**, ships and defences **+10%**, Plasma Mastery counted **one and a half times**.

## Exploration

*Search faster, reach further.*

| Row | Node | Type | Effect (full node) | Per rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Method** | 3 ranks | Your research progresses 15% faster. | 3 × 5% |
| 2 | **Cartographers** | 5 ranks | Your expeditions spend 25% less time exploring: the exploration time you choose is shortened by as much. | 5 × 5% |
| 3 | **A · Ark** | choice | Your colony ships cost 25% less and build 50% faster; your colonisations fly 25% faster. | |
|  | **B · Young Worlds** |  | A founded colony stays young 3 days longer. | |
| 4 | **Derelict Raiders** | 5 ranks | Against an inactive player, a ruined world or a masterless empire, you plunder 10 pts more resources. | 5 × 2 pts |
| 5 | **Surveyors** | key | Each of your planets gains 2 buildable fields. |  |
| 6 | **A · Expeditions** | choice | Your expeditions bring back 20% more. | |
|  | **B · Astrometry** |  | Your espionage and astrophysics research progresses 15% faster. | |
| 7 | **Pioneer Outposts** | 5 ranks | While a colony is young, it builds 20% faster and its mines produce 25% more, and it stays young 5 days longer. | 5 × 4% |
| 8 | **A · Stolen Archives** | choice | Each profitable plunder of an abandoned target advances your current research by 1% of its remaining time (10 times a day). | |
|  | **B · Prospector's Nose** |  | 33% of your expeditions that find nothing bring back resources instead. | |
| 9 | **Organised Plunder** | 5 ranks | Against an inactive player, a ruined world or a masterless empire, you plunder 10 pts more resources. | 5 × 2 pts |
| 10 | **Second Innovation Center** | ultimate | Second Innovation Center: you can run two studies at once; the second progresses at 50% of the normal speed. |  |

- **Method** and **Astrometry** share the same 25% cap: with Method full, Tactical Intelligence and Applied Cosmology go 25% faster, not 30%.
- **Cartographers** shortens the exploration stay you choose. The trip does not change, and the stay's hydrogen, the delay risk and the gains remain those of the chosen duration.
- **Ark** has three effects on the Space Pioneer and colonisation. Colonisation speed stays under the fleet speed total cap (60%).
- A colony is **young** for 7 days after being founded by a Space Pioneer (never the home planet, a moon or a conquered world). Only **Pioneer Outposts** uses that state: **Young Worlds** only works with it. With both: 15 days of youth.
- An **abandoned target** is a ruined world, an opponent controlled by the game, or a player inactive in the universe. The base plunder rate is 50%; Derelict Raiders and Organised Plunder add points to that rate.
- **Stolen Archives** only counts plunders that bring back at least one hour of your home planet's production (the three resources added together). It advances the current study (the first one if two are running), never to completion; the 10 charges are counted over 24 hours from the first one.
- **Prospector's Nose**: 33% of the chance of finding nothing moves to resources. On a deep-space cell never explored before, "nothing" goes from 33% to 22.11%, "resources" from 33% to 43.89%.
- **Second Innovation Center**: the second study must differ from the first and only starts while a study is already running. At half speed, a 4 h study takes 8 h.

Full branch: research **+15%** and a second study, expedition stays **25%** shorter, plunder of abandoned targets **70%**, **+2 fields**, young colonies faster and young for up to **12 days** (**15** with Young Worlds).

## Logistics

*Further, faster, fuller.*

| Row | Node | Type | Effect (full node) | Per rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tuned Reactors** | 3 ranks | All your fleets fly 15% faster. | 3 × 5% |
| 2 | **Economisers** | 5 ranks | Your fleets burn 10% less hydrogen. | 5 × 2% |
| 3 | **A · Wider Holds** | choice | Your ships carry 10% more cargo. | |
|  | **B · Pushed Engines** |  | All your fleets fly 10% faster. | |
| 4 | **Modular Holds** | 5 ranks | Your ships carry 15% more cargo. | 5 × 3% |
| 5 | **Extra Squadron** | key | You can have 1 more fleet in flight at the same time. |  |
| 6 | **A · Tailwind** | choice | Your fleets come home 25% faster. | |
|  | **B · Inner Lanes** |  | Your transports and deployments between your own planets travel 40% faster. | |
| 7 | **Advanced Propulsion** | 5 ranks | All your fleets fly 15% faster. | 5 × 3% |
| 8 | **Priority Loading** | key | What does not fit in your holds is loaded in the order you choose. |  |
| 9 | **Resupply** | 5 ranks | Your fleets burn 10% less hydrogen. | 5 × 2% |
| 10 | **Grand Fleet** | ultimate | You can have 1 more fleet in flight at the same time. |  |

- Speed bonuses **divide** the flight duration: +40% = a flight 1.4 times shorter. They are read when the fleet leaves.
- Hydrogen reductions apply to the flight's fuel, not to an expedition stay's. Cargo adds to the capacity already raised by Hyperspace Technology.
- **Tailwind** speeds up every way home, recalls included. **Inner Lanes** speeds up transports and deployments arriving at one of your planets. Both multiply the speed on top of the speed cap.
- **Priority Loading**: when leaving, you set the order in which plunder and debris fill your holds when everything does not fit. Without it, each resource is loaded in proportion.

Full branch: flights **30%** faster and cargo **+25%** (option A in row 3), or flights **40%** faster and cargo **+15%** (option B); hydrogen **−20%**; **+2** fleets; Priority Loading.

## Worked example

**Full Prosperity, Plasma Mastery level 10**
- A metal mine producing 10,000 per hour (raw production) gains +25% from talents, that is 2,500.
- Plasma 10 gives +10%; with Plasma Foundry, it counts as 15 levels: +15%, that is 1,500.
- Total: **14,000** per hour (without the Foundry: 13,500), before the energy ratio. With The Halden Rotation: 14,200.
- An 8 h building is built in 8 / 1.25 = **6 h 24 min**.

**Exploration down to Astrometry**
- A 10 h study completes in 10 / 1.15 = **8 h 41 min 45 s**.
- Applied Cosmology, 10 h with full Method and Astrometry: the 25% cap applies, **8 h**.
- An expedition exploring for 4 h now only explores for **3 h** (Cartographers at rank 5). The trip does not change.

**Plundering an inactive player** (base plunder rate 50%, see [Plunder](/en/combat/plunder))
- They have 100,000 metal in stock: you take 50,000 without talents, **70,000** with full Derelict Raiders and Organised Plunder (+20 points), within your holds' limit.

**Full Logistics, option B**
- A 1 h flight takes 60 / 1.4 = **42 min 51 s**. With Tailwind instead of Inner Lanes, the way home takes 42 min 51 s / 1.25 = **34 min 17 s**.
- A flight burning 10,000 hydrogen only burns **8,000**.
- A Stellar Freighter with 25,000 cargo carries 25,000 × 1.15 = **28,750** resources (without Hyperspace Technology).

### What 50 points buy

No build takes everything: each one chooses where its speed goes. The four splits below respect the gates.

| Build | Split | What it gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Industrialist** | Prosperity 28, Exploration 22 (down to row 8) | mines +25%, buildings +25%, ships +10%, Plasma ×1.5; research +15%, stays 25% shorter, plunder of abandoned targets 60%, +2 fields, faster young colonies |
| **Raider** | Logistics 28, Exploration 22 | flights 30 to 40% faster, hydrogen −20%, cargo +15 to 25%, +2 fleets; research +15%, stays 25% shorter, plunder of abandoned targets 60% |
| **Scholar** | Exploration 28, Prosperity 22 | research +15% and a second study, stays 25% shorter, plunder of abandoned targets 70%; mines +24%, buildings +10%, ships +10%, Plasma ×1.5 |
| **Generalist** | rows 1 to 6 of the three branches (48 points) and 2 points in row 7 | mines +24%, buildings +10%, Plasma ×1.5; research +15%, stays 25% shorter, plunder of abandoned targets 60%, +2 fields; flights 15 to 25% faster, hydrogen −10%, +1 fleet |

The caps of each bonus, with alliance talents, are on the [Talents](/en/dynasty/talents#caps-of-the-shared-bonuses) page.

## Common pitfalls

- **Reading "+40% speed" as a flight 40% shorter**: speed divides the duration. A 1 h flight becomes 42 min 51 s, not 36 min.
- **Taking Young Worlds alone**: without Pioneer Outposts, a young colony has no bonus, and extending it does nothing.
- **Adding up Method and Astrometry**: both fit within the same 25% cap.
- **Cartographers and the gains**: the talent shortens the exploration, but the expedition brings back as if it had explored for the chosen duration.
- **Derelict Raiders against an active player**: the bonus only works against inactive players, ruined worlds and opponents controlled by the game.
- **Stolen Archives and small plunders**: a plunder bringing back less than one hour of your home planet's production does not count.
- **Two ultimates**: two full branches require 56 points, beyond the 50 of the budget.
