# The common tree

> **In short**: the common tree is the same for every dynasty and every class. It gets 1 point per level, 50 at most, and sets your own pace of play: **Prosperity** to produce and build, **Exploration** to research and expand, **Logistics** for your fleets. 50 points are enough for a full branch and most of a second one.
{.is-info}

> Ship names are provisional English translations: the game still shows French names (see the [glossary](/en/getting-started/glossary)).
{.is-warning}

## Rules

1. 3 branches of 10 rows, 28 points per branch, 84 in total. You place **50 at most**.
2. Rows open at 8, 20 and 32 points placed above, all branches together. The ultimate (row 10) also requires level 40 and the full branch. See [Talents](/en/dynasty/talents).
3. Rows 3, 6 and 8 are **choices**: one point, option A or B.
4. The common tree **does not change** when you change class.
5. The bonuses that only serve you (production, research, construction) climb high. Those that touch other players (fleet speed, plunder) stay moderate.
6. Fleet speed bonuses **divide the flight time**: +50% = a flight 1.5 times shorter.

## Prosperity

*What your worlds produce and build.*

| Row | Node | Effect | Per rank | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Extraction** | Production of the three mines | 3 ranks × 3% | +9% |
| 2 | **Automated Cranes** | Building construction speed | 5 ranks × 2% | +10% |
| 3 | **A · Warehouses** or **B · Buried Vaults** | A: storage capacity <br> B: share of your resources a raider cannot carry off | | A: +20% <br> B: 5 points (a raider takes 45% instead of 50%) |
| 4 | **Rich Veins** | Production of the three mines | 5 ranks × 3% | +15% (+24% with Extraction) |
| 5 | **Plasma Foundry** | Effect of Plasma Technology on production | | +20% of its effect |
| 6 | **A · Optimised Grid** or **B · Thriving Colonies** | A: energy of the Photovoltaic Sensors, the Thermonuclear Reactor and the Solar Collectors <br> B: production of your colonies, home planet excluded | | A: +8% <br> B: +10% |
| 7 | **Workshops** | Ship and defence construction speed | 5 ranks × 2% | +10% |
| 8 | **A · Resource Bridge** or **B · Safety Stock** | A: once a day, transfer to another of your planets, instantly and without a fleet, up to 2 hours of the departure planet's production <br> B: with full storage, your mines keep producing at 25% instead of stopping | | A: 2 h, once a day <br> B: 25% |
| 9 | **Night Shifts** | Building construction speed | 5 ranks × 2% | +10% (+20% with Automated Cranes) |
| 10 | **Golden Age** *(ultimate)* | Mine production and building speed | | mines +1% (+25% in total), buildings +5% (+25% in total) |

Full branch: mines **+25%**, buildings **+25%**, ships and defences **+10%**, Plasma Technology **+20%** of its effect.

## Exploration

*Search faster, reach further.*

| Row | Node | Effect | Per rank | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Method** | Research speed | 3 ranks × 5% | +15% |
| 2 | **Cartographers** | Exploration time of an expedition. The outbound and return flights, hydrogen, the risk of delay and the gains stay computed on the time you chose: the talent shortens the wait, not the gains | 5 ranks × 5% | −25% |
| 3 | **A · Ark** or **B · Young Worlds** | A: cost of the Space Pioneer <br> B: how long a founded colony stays "young" | | A: −25% <br> B: +72 h (10 days instead of 7) |
| 4 | **Derelict Raiders** | Plunder against an inactive player, a ruined world or a game-controlled opponent | 5 ranks × 2 points | +10 points (50 → 60%) |
| 5 | **Surveyors** | Buildable fields on each of your planets | | +2 |
| 6 | **A · Expeditions** or **B · Astrometry** | A: what your expeditions bring back <br> B: speed of Espionage Technology and Astrophysics | | A: +20% <br> B: +15% |
| 7 | **Pioneer Outposts** | While a founded colony is young: its building speed and its mine production | 5 ranks × 4% and 5% | buildings +20%, mines +25% |
| 8 | **A · Stolen Archives** or **B · Prospector's Nose** | A: each profitable plunder of an abandoned target advances your current research by 1% of its remaining time, 10 times a day <br> B: a share of the expeditions that find nothing bring back resources instead | | A: 1%, 10 a day <br> B: 33% |
| 9 | **Organised Plunder** | Plunder of abandoned targets | 5 ranks × 2 points | +10 points (+20 points with Derelict Raiders: 50 → 70%) |
| 10 | **Second Laboratory** *(ultimate)* | You run **two studies at once**, the second at half speed | | 2nd study at 50% |

Full branch: research **+15%** and a second study, expeditions **25%** shorter, faster young colonies, **+2 fields**, plunder of abandoned targets **70%**.

## Logistics

*Further, faster, fuller.*

| Row | Node | Effect | Per rank | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tuned Reactors** | Speed of all your fleets | 3 ranks × 5% | +15% |
| 2 | **Economisers** | Hydrogen burnt | 5 ranks × 2% | −10% |
| 3 | **A · Wider Holds** or **B · Pushed Engines** | A: cargo capacity <br> B: speed of all your fleets | | A: +10% <br> B: +10% |
| 4 | **Modular Holds** | Cargo capacity | 5 ranks × 3% | +15% (+25% with Wider Holds) |
| 5 | **Extra Squadron** | Fleet slots | | +1 |
| 6 | **A · Tailwind** or **B · Inner Lanes** | A: speed of all your returns <br> B: speed of transports and stationings between your own planets | | A: +25% <br> B: +40% <br> outside the speed cap |
| 7 | **Advanced Propulsion** | Speed of all your fleets | 5 ranks × 3% | +15% (+30% with Tuned Reactors, +40% with Pushed Engines) |
| 8 | **Priority Loading** | What does not fit in your holds is loaded in the order you choose | | unlock |
| 9 | **Resupply** | Hydrogen burnt | 5 ranks × 2% | −10% (−20% with Economisers) |
| 10 | **Grand Fleet** *(ultimate)* | Fleet slots | | +1 (+2 in total) |

Full branch: flights **30%** faster and cargo **+25%** (option A), or flights **40%** faster and cargo **+15%** (option B); hydrogen **−20%**; **+2** fleets.

## Worked example

**Full Prosperity**
- A Mineral Excavator at 10,000 metal per hour rises to **12,500** per hour (+25%).
- An 8 h building is built in 8 / 1.25 = **6 h 24**.

**Exploration up to Method and Cartographers**
- A 10 h study finishes in 10 / 1.15 = **8 h 42**.
- An expedition exploring for 4 h only explores for **3 h** (Cartographers at rank 5). The flight does not change.

**Full Logistics, option B**
- A 1 h flight lasts 60 / 1.4 = **42 min 51 s**.
- A flight burning 10,000 hydrogen only burns **8,000**.
- A Stellar Freighter carries 25,000 × 1.15 = **28,750** resources.

## Detailed data

### What 50 points buy

No build takes everything: each chooses where its speed goes.

| Build | Split | What it gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Industrialist** | Prosperity 28, Exploration 22 (down to row 8) | mines +25%, buildings +25%, ships +10%, plasma +20% of its effect; research +15%, expeditions 25% shorter, faster young colonies, +2 fields |
| **Raider** | Logistics 28, Exploration 22 | flights 30 to 40% faster, hydrogen −20%, cargo +15 to 25%, +2 fleets; research +15%, expeditions 25% shorter, plunder of abandoned targets 60% |
| **Scholar** | Exploration 28, Prosperity 22 | research +15% and a second study, expeditions 25% shorter, plunder of abandoned targets 70%; mines +24%, buildings +10%, plasma +20% of its effect |
| **Generalist** | about 16 per branch | mines +24%, buildings +10%; research +15%, expeditions 25% shorter; flights 15 to 25% faster, hydrogen −10%, +1 fleet |

The caps of each bonus, with alliance talents, are on the [Talents](/en/dynasty/talents#caps-of-the-shared-bonuses) page.

## Common pitfalls

- **Reading "+40% speed" as a flight 40% shorter**: speed divides the duration. A 1 h flight drops to 42 min 51 s, not to 36 min.
- **Cartographers and the gains**: the talent shortens the exploration, but the expedition brings back as if it had explored for the chosen time.
- **Derelict Raiders against an active player**: the bonus only applies against inactive players, ruined worlds and game-controlled opponents.
- **Two ultimates**: two full branches take 56 points, beyond the 50 of the budget.
