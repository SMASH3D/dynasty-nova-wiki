# Talents

> **In short**: you have two trees. The **common tree** gets 1 point per level, 50 at most, and carries the generic bonuses. The **class tree** gets 1 point per 2 mastery levels, 25 at most, and carries your class's mechanics. You prepare your choices as a draft, then you confirm. A confirmed point only comes back through a reset.
{.is-info}

> Ship names are provisional English translations: the game still shows French names (see the [glossary](/en/getting-started/glossary)).
{.is-warning}

## Rules

### Two trees, two pools
1. The **common tree**: 3 branches of 10 rows, 28 points per branch. It gets **1 point per level** of your character, **50 at most**.
2. The **class tree**: 3 branches of 9 rows, 15 points per branch. It gets **1 point per 2 mastery levels** of your class, rounded up, **25 at most**. See [Class mastery](/en/dynasty/classes#class-mastery).
3. The two pools are **separate**: a common point never goes into the class tree, and the other way round.

### Nodes and rows
4. Each row of a branch holds one node. Three kinds of nodes:
   - **ranked**: each point adds a rank (3 ranks in the class tree, 3 or 5 in the common tree);
   - **choice**: a single point, two options **A** and **B**, only one at a time;
   - **key**: a single point, one mechanic.
5. The last row of each branch is an **ultimate**. Each tree has three ultimates, and your budget allows **one**.

### Gates
6. A row opens when the rows above it hold enough points, **all branches together**.

| Tree | Rows 1 to 3 | Rows 4 to 6 | Rows 7 and 8 | Rows 9 and 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | open | 8 points | 20 points | 32 points |
| Class | open | 5 points | 12 points (rows 7 to 9) | |

7. An **ultimate** also requires **level 40** and its **full branch**.

### Draft and confirmation
8. You place and remove your points freely as a **draft**. Nothing applies until you click **Confirm**.
9. A confirmed point is **final**: it only comes back through a reset.

### Reset
10. Each tree is reset **separately** and gives back all its points.
11. The **first** reset of each tree is **free**. The next ones cost **20 Stellar Points** and require a **24-hour** wait since the previous one.
12. A [class change](/en/dynasty/classes#changing-class) empties the class tree and makes its next reset free. The common tree does not move.

### Caps
13. Each bonus has a **cap for talents** and a **total cap**, alliance talents included. A point that would go past a cap adds nothing.
14. A class may push a common bonus further, but only on a **narrow scope** (a mission type, a family of ships or buildings). That is an **accent**, with a cap of its own.
15. Fleet speed bonuses (talents and alliance) **divide the flight time**: +50% speed = a flight 1.5 times shorter. See [Movement](/en/fleet/movement).

## Worked example

**At level 20, with a class played since the start**
- Common tree: **20 points**. Rows 7 and 8 have just opened.
- Class tree: mastery 20, so **10 points**. Rows 4 to 6 are open, rows 7 to 9 wait for 12 points.

**A full common branch**
- It costs 28 points. Its rows 1 to 8 hold 22, but row 9 requires 32 points above it: you must have placed **10 elsewhere** before finishing it.
- At level 50, 22 points are left for a second branch, enough to reach its row 8, without its ultimate.
- In the class tree, rows 1 to 8 of a branch hold 14 points: the 12-point gate opens without placing anything elsewhere. With 25 points, 10 are left for a second branch, down to its row 6.

**A reset**
- First reset of the common tree: free.
- Second one, the next day: 20 Stellar Points, possible 24 hours after the first.

## Detailed data

### Points per level

Experience comes from quests and from your points record: **10 experience per point** of [ranking](/en/players/rankings), in a universe at speed ×1.

> **Universe setting**: the experience per ranking point is divided by the universe speed.
> Default: **10 per point** at speed ×1 · Redline: **10 per point**
{.is-info}

| Level | Total experience | Common points | Class points (same mastery) | What opens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 | common and class, rows 1 to 3 |
| 8 | 500 | 8 | 4 | common, rows 4 to 6 |
| 10 | 1,221 | 10 | 5 | class, rows 4 to 6 |
| 20 | 43,939 | 20 | 10 | common, rows 7 and 8 |
| 24 | 100,397 | 24 | 12 | class, rows 7 to 9 |
| 32 | 437,052 | 32 | 16 | common, rows 9 and 10 |
| 40 | 1,773,675 | 40 | 20 | ultimates (with the full branch) |
| 50 | 10,000,000 | 50 | 25 | the whole budget |

Past level 50, experience keeps counting but gives no more points.

## Common pitfalls

- **Confirming too fast**: a confirmed point does not come back without a reset, and only the first one is free.
- **Aiming for two ultimates**: two full branches take 56 common points or 30 class points. The budget allows one.
- **Stacking a bonus past its cap**: the extra points give nothing. Check the cap before adding an alliance talent on the same bonus.
- **Reading +50% speed as a flight twice as short**: speed divides the duration. +50% turns a 1 h flight into 40 min.
