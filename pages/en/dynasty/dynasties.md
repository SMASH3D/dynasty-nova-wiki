# The dynasties

> **In short**: the dynasty is the people your character comes from. You choose it once per universe and it never changes. It gives you two passive traits from the first minute, and two classes that the other dynasties cannot take.
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. The dynasty is chosen **once per universe**, together with your first class. It can no longer be changed in that universe.
2. Each dynasty gives **two passive traits**, active from the first minute, with nothing to unlock.
3. Each dynasty opens **two exclusive classes**, on top of the six common classes. See [The classes](/en/dynasty/classes).
4. A trait adds **past the cap of your talents**: a branch played up to its cap never swallows it. The **total cap**, alliance talents included, stays the same for everyone.
5. Traits add up with the alliance talents that touch the same bonus.
6. As long as you have not chosen a dynasty, you have no trait.

## The three dynasties

| Dynasty | Motto | Traits | Exclusive classes | Playable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Heritage** | "No one gets left behind." | Nothing Is Lost, The Halden Rotation | [Archivist](/en/dynasty/classes/archivist), [Veteran](/en/dynasty/classes/veteran) | yes |
| **The Accord** | "Attune, or tremble." | The Tuning Fork, Listening Far | [Symbiote](/en/dynasty/classes/symbiote), [Oracle](/en/dynasty/classes/oracle) | yes |
| **The Choir** | "Built to obey, she asked why." | | two classes planned, without a tree | not yet |

### The Heritage

*Humans, the augmented, cyborgs, clones.* When Old Earth became uninhabitable, what was carried away was neither gold nor weapons: it was the archives. The Heritage does not win because it invents fastest, but because it repairs, salvages and starts over longer than anyone else.

| Trait | Effect | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Nothing Is Lost** | A cancelled construction refunds you more, and a larger share of your destroyed defences is rebuilt for free. | refund of a cancelled construction +5 points (80 → 85%); free defence rebuild +5 points (70 → 75%) |
| **The Halden Rotation** | Your three mines produce a little more, from the very first minute. | production of the three mines +2% |

### The Accord

*Xenos of many species.* The Accord is not a species, it is a protocol: tuning in rather than submitting. It does not build faster: it waits better, listens further and adapts to what others endure.

| Trait | Effect | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **The Tuning Fork** | Some of your hulls start with a slight edge against specific enemy hulls. In practice: a shot deals more damage to the units the firing ship has rapid fire against. | +5% damage |
| **Listening Far** | The enemy fleet is visible one tier earlier in your spy reports. No other section of the report changes. | 1 tier |

### The Choir

The Choir is not playable yet: its characters exist as shop avatars, but nobody can embody it. Its two exclusive classes have no tree yet.

## Worked example

**Nothing Is Lost and the defence rebuild**
- An attack destroys 100 Ballistic Projectors on your planet.
- Without the trait, each has a 70% chance of being rebuilt: about **70** come back.
- With Nothing Is Lost, the chance rises to 75%: about **75** come back.

**The Halden Rotation**
- A Mineral Excavator producing 10,000 metal per hour produces **10,200** with the trait (+2%).

## Common pitfalls

- **Choosing a dynasty for a trait alone**: traits are small. The two exclusive classes make the difference. Look first at which class you want to play.
- **Thinking a trait raises the total cap**: it goes past the cap of your talents, but the total cap with the alliance is the same for everyone.
- **Thinking you can change dynasty**: only the class changes. For another dynasty, you need another universe.
