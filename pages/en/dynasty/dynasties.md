# The dynasties

> **In short**: the dynasty is the people your character comes from. You choose it once per universe and it never changes there. It gives you two passive traits from the very first minute, and two classes the other dynasties cannot take.
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. The dynasty is chosen **once per universe**. It cannot be changed in that universe afterwards.
2. Each dynasty grants **two passive traits**, active from the first minute, with nothing to unlock.
3. Each dynasty opens **two exclusive classes**, on top of the six common classes. See [The classes](/en/dynasty/classes).
4. A trait moves the **starting value** of a bonus, never its **cap**: everyone's talents then climb to the same cap.
5. Traits add up with the alliance talents that touch the same bonus.
6. Until you have chosen a dynasty, you have no trait.

## The three dynasties

| Dynasty | Motto | Traits | Exclusive classes | Playable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Heritage** | "No one gets left behind." | Nothing Is Lost, The Halden Rotation | [Archivist](/en/dynasty/classes/archivist), [Veteran](/en/dynasty/classes/veteran) | yes |
| **The Accord** | "Attune, or tremble." | The Tuning Fork, Listening Far | [Symbiote](/en/dynasty/classes/symbiote), [Oracle](/en/dynasty/classes/oracle) | yes |
| **The Choir** | "Built to obey, she asked why." | | Swarm, Remanence | not yet |

### The Heritage

*Humans, augmented, cyborgs, clones.* When Old Earth became uninhabitable, what was carried away was neither gold nor weapons: it was the archives. The Heritage does not win because it invents fastest, but because it repairs, salvages and starts again for longer than anyone else.

| Trait | Effect | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Nothing Is Lost** | A cancelled construction refunds you more, and a larger share of your destroyed defences is rebuilt for free. | cancellation refund +5 points (80 → 85%); defence rebuild +5 points (70 → 75%) |
| **The Halden Rotation** | Your three mines produce a little more, from the very first minute. | +2% |

### The Accord

*Xenos of many species.* The Accord is not a species, it is a protocol: attuning rather than submitting. It does not build faster: it waits better, listens further and adapts to what others endure.

| Trait | Effect | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **The Tuning Fork** | Your shots deal more damage to the units the firing ship has rapid fire against. | +5% |
| **Listening Far** | The enemy fleet can be read one tier earlier in your spy reports. No other section changes. | 1 tier |

### The Choir

The Choir is not playable yet: its characters exist as shop avatars, but nobody can embody it. Its two classes, Swarm and Remanence, have no tree yet.

## Worked example

**Nothing Is Lost and the defence rebuild**
- An attack destroys 100 Ballistic Projectors on your planet.
- Without the trait, each has a 70% chance of being rebuilt: about **70** come back.
- With Nothing Is Lost, the chance rises to 75%: about **75** come back.

**The Halden Rotation**
- A Mineral Excavator producing 10,000 metal per hour produces **10,200** with the trait.

## Common pitfalls

- **Choosing a dynasty for a trait alone**: the traits are small. The two exclusive classes make the difference. Look first at the class you want to play.
- **Thinking a trait raises a cap**: a Heritage player starts at 75% defence rebuild, but the cap stays the same for everyone.
- **Believing you can change dynasty**: only the class changes. Another dynasty means another universe.
