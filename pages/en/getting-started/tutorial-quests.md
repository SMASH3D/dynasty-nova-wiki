# The tutorial (quests)

> **In short**: the quest campaign guides you step by step, from your first mine to advanced defenses. It has **102 quests spread over 7 grades**. Each quest gives a resource pack and experience. It is the best way to get started: the team recommends letting the quests guide you.
{.is-info}

> Ship names are provisional English translations: the game still shows French names (see the [glossary](/en/getting-started/glossary)).
{.is-warning}

## Rules

1. Quests are grouped into **7 grades**. All quests of a grade are available at once.
2. The next grade **unlocks as a whole** once every quest of the current grade is done.
3. A quest is done as soon as its goal is reached (a building or research level, a unit built, a colonization, a recycling run). You then have to **claim** the reward.
4. **Rewards**: a resource pack, stored in your inventory, that you use whenever you want on the planet of your choice. Plus experience.
5. Completing a whole grade gives an **experience bonus**.
6. Experience is tracked per universe. It raises your character's level, which gives your talent points. See [Dynasty and talents](/en/dynasty).
7. Quests give no Stellar Points. There is no grade after the 7th.

## Worked example

**Grade 1** has 9 quests. Together they give:
- **2,634 metal, 859 crystal and 200 hydrogen**, as packs to use wherever you like;
- **71 experience points**, plus a **29** bonus for completing the grade, so **100**.

## Common pitfalls

- **Claim your rewards**: a completed quest pays nothing until you claim it.
- **Packs wait in the inventory**: use them on the planet that needs them most, ideally before your storage is full.
- **Energy follows mines**: quests raise mines quickly. Keep your Photovoltaic Sensors up to date to avoid an energy shortage.
- **Grade 5 asks for a colonization**: plan a colony slot (Astrophysics 1) and a Space Pioneer.
