# FAQ

> **In short**: short answers to the most common questions, with a link to the detailed page.
{.is-info}

## Getting started

**Where do I start?**
Follow the quests: they make you build in the right order and give you resources. See [The tutorial](/en/getting-started/tutorial-quests).

**Why did my production drop?**
Almost always because of energy: if your mines use more than your power plants produce, all output drops. Add a power plant rather than a mine. See [Energy](/en/economy/energy).

**My metal production has stopped.**
Your stock has probably exceeded your storage capacity. See [Storage](/en/economy/storage).

## Attacking and defending

**Why can't I attack this player?**
Several possible reasons: they are in their first 7 days, the points gap is too wide (either way), they are on vacation, or you have already used your 6 attacks in 24 hours against them. See [Beginner protection](/en/players/beginner-protection) and [Attack limit](/en/players/attack-limit).

**My attack ended in a draw: why was nothing plundered?**
Plunder only happens on a victory. See [Plunder](/en/combat/plunder).

**Do my destroyed defenses come back?**
Each has a 70% chance of being repaired for free, unless it was destroyed by a missile. See [Defense rebuild](/en/combat/defense-rebuild).

**Do debris fields disappear?**
No, never. They must be recycled with Salvagers. See [Debris fields](/en/combat/debris).

## Fleets and colonies

**How many colonies can I have?**
It depends on Astrophysics: 2 planets at level 1, then one more every two levels. See [Colonization](/en/universe/colonization).

**Why can't I send more fleets?**
Your fleet slots are full: you have 1 + your Quantum Computing level, expeditions included. See [Fleet movement](/en/fleet/movement).

**Can I make a grouped attack or defend an ally?**
No: these missions do not exist. See [Missions](/en/fleet/missions).

**Does staying longer on an expedition raise the odds?**
No, duration does not change the odds. See [Expeditions](/en/fleet/expeditions).

## Level and talents

**Where does the experience that raises my level and my class mastery come from?**
From two sources only: the quests you claim, and your points record in the rankings, at 10 experience points per point (divided by the universe's economy speed). Building, researching, producing ships and defenses raises your points, hence your experience. Winning a battle, plundering or recycling gives none directly, and losing units takes none away. Class mastery rises with the same experience. See [Talents](/en/dynasty/talents#points-per-level) and [The classes](/en/dynasty/classes#class-mastery).

## Account

**I am going away for a long time: what should I do?**
Turn on vacation mode: nobody can attack you, and your inactivity does not count. See [Vacation mode](/en/players/vacation-mode).

**Do Stellar Points give an advantage?**
In PvP universes, the shop only sells cosmetics; Stellar Points also pay for planet renaming and Premium. See [Premium, shop and Stellar Points](/en/misc/premium).
