# Coming from OGame?

> **In short**: Dynasty Nova builds on the space strategy basics you know, but many rules change: no officers, no dark matter, no alliance combat system, reworked espionage, simpler plunder, more protections and configurable universes. This page lists what stands out.
{.is-info}

> Ship names are provisional English translations: the game still shows French names (see the [glossary](/en/getting-started/glossary)).
{.is-warning}

## Rules

### What does not exist
- **Pay-to-win**: in PvP universes, no purchase gives resources, output or speed-ups. It is a strong choice, the opposite of officers and dark matter.
- **Grouped attacks (ACS) and allied defense**: no mission carries them, and you cannot station at an ally's.
- **Officers**: replaced by a single Premium subscription.
- **Dark matter**: replaced by Stellar Points, which buy no gameplay advantage in PvP universes.
- **Merchant, marketplace, resource trading**.
- **Player and alliance classes**.
- **Lifeforms, items, boosters, planet relocation**.
- **Pathfinder, Reaper, Crawler**: the fleet has the 14 classic ships, Battlecruiser and Deathstar included.
- **Drive switching** with research: every ship keeps its original drive.
- **Circular map**: distances never wrap around the galaxy edge.
- **Account deletion for inactivity**: a single threshold, which only lifts beginner protection.

### What is new
- **Hidden positions**: in the galaxy view, a position stays unreadable until it has been spied on. An exotic script hides the planet's name and its owner's.
- **Illustrated planets**: each planet has a type and a biome that set its look, in list, orbit and 3D views. **Planet skins** let you customise yours.
- **Private universes**: you can open your own paid universe, with its players, speeds and rules (see [Private universes](/en/misc/private-universes)).
- **Deep space**: a cell after the last position of each system, the only destination for expeditions. Systems have **9 to 20 positions** depending on the universe.
- **Planetary Base** (Colonial Base on a colony): a building that cannot be demolished, providing base storage and a small fixed income.
- **Five independent universe speeds**: economy, buildings, research, shipyard, fleets.
- **Configurable universes**: protection tiers, anti-bashing, debris rates, moon formation, abandonment, queue size, bookmarks.
- **New player protection**: 7 days on arrival, lost on your first raid, on top of points-based protection.
- **Repair Station**: recovers a share of the ships destroyed while defending, with no dice roll.
- **Voluntary planet abandonment**: the planet stays on the map and decays for 60 days, then disappears.
- **Richer alliances**: level and experience, 14 talents, shared treasury, alliance missions, pacts, wars declared by vote, automatic succession of an absent founder.
- **A 102-quest campaign** in 7 grades.
- **Built-in chat** with three channels (general, alliance, private), annotated bookmarks.
- **Maintenance = truce**: attacks, espionage and missiles suspended everywhere.
- **Replayable battles**: every battle is deterministic and can be replayed identically.

### What works differently
- **Plunder**: 50% of each resource, loaded in proportion to cargo space. No one-third rule.
- **Anti-bashing**: 6 attacks per 24 h on a given player, missiles included, fixed window opened by the first attack. The 7th is refused at launch, and a recall gives the slot back.
- **Debris**: never expires. 30% of ships, 0% of defenses, 10% for expedition battles.
- **Defense repair**: a 70% roll per unit.
- **Cancellation**: 80% of the cost, pro rata of the remaining time. **Demolition** is free, takes half the build time and refunds nothing.
- **Build queues**: four queues (buildings, research, ships, defenses) of 2 orders, 5 with Premium. An order is paid only when it starts, and dropped at no cost if the planet cannot pay.
- **Beginner protection**: configurable, symmetric point tiers (the stronger player is protected too), lifted after an inactivity period set per universe (7 days on Redline).
- **Espionage**: detail level = Espionage Technology gap + a probe bonus capped at +5, with no squaring. Counter-espionage is a single roll (5 to 25%) for the whole wave, whatever the number of probes.
- **Sensor phalanx**: 5,000 hydrogen per scan, unchanged range.
- **Solar satellite** (Solar Collector): output tied to maximum temperature; the solar plant gets a bonus tied to current temperature.
- **Galaxy size**: the number of systems per galaxy depends on the universe (99 on Redline, against a fixed 499 in OGame).
- **Position bonus**: colonies only (crystal at positions 1 to 3, metal at positions 6 to 10), never on the mother planet.
- **Living temperature**: it swings every month between the planet's minimum and maximum.
- **Expeditions**: no dark matter or merchant; pirates and aliens fought by the real combat engine; cap set by the universe's best player; areas that wear out; each hour on site beyond the first adds 15% to the find.
- **Moon formation**: chance and threshold configurable per universe.
- **Vacation mode**: 48 h minimum, no maximum; espionage and recycling remain possible, with an empty report.
- **Battlecruiser** (Predator): also requires Ion Technology 5.
- **Warp factor** shown on ships: no effect.

## Worked example

**Spying with 10 probes**
- Probe bonus: whole part of (10 / 2) = 5, the maximum.
- To see buildings (tier 7), you therefore need **2 more Espionage Technology levels** than the target; for research (tier 9), **4 levels**.
- Sending 20 probes adds nothing, and does not change the risk of losing them.

## Common pitfalls

- **"Scout" is not the Pathfinder**: in Dynasty Nova, the Scout is the espionage probe.
- **Cancelling is costly**: you only get back 80% of the cost, pro rata of the remaining time.
- **The stronger player is protected too**: you cannot attack a much stronger player, not only a much weaker one.
- **No ACS**: do not plan a grouped attack, it does not exist.
- **Your ships' drive never changes**: do not count on a propulsion research to speed up your small cargo ships.
