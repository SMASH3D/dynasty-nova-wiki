# Plunder

> **In short**: when the attacker wins a battle, it takes **50% of each resource** stored on the target planet or moon, within the free cargo space of its surviving ships. If the cargo space is too small, each resource is reduced in the same proportion. This rate is the same in every universe.
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. Plunder only happens **when the attacker wins**. A draw or a defeat brings nothing.
2. The target's stock is first brought up to the moment of arrival: production built up since the player's last visit counts.
3. The loot is **50% of each resource** (metal, crystal, hydrogen), rounded down. Energy cannot be plundered.
4. The loot is capped by the **free cargo space of surviving ships**: their cargo capacity, increased by **5% per level** of Hyperspace Technology, minus what is already on board.
5. If the loot exceeds the cargo space, **each resource is reduced in the same proportion** (rounded down). There is no priority order between resources.
6. The target loses exactly what was carried away.
7. The rate does not drop from one attack to the next: the target's stock does. Plundering the same player several times therefore brings less and less if they do not produce in between.
8. A **moon** is plundered like a planet: the moon's own stock counts.
9. Buildings, ships and defenses cannot be plundered.

### What changes plunder
10. [Dynasty and class talents](/en/dynasty/talents) can change these rules: the defender can shelter part of its stock (the rate it suffers never goes below **30%**); the attacker can plunder more from an abandoned planet, plunder a little on a draw, enlarge its holds or choose the loading order.

## Worked example

The target stores 100,000 metal, 60,000 crystal and 20,000 hydrogen.
- Possible loot: 50,000 metal, 30,000 crystal, 10,000 hydrogen, so **90,000**.
- With 10 surviving Stellar Freighters (250,000 cargo, 300,000 with Hyperspace Technology 4): everything is taken.
- With only 50 Assailants (5,000 cargo): the holds take only 5,000 out of 90,000.
- With 45,000 free cargo space, half the loot: **25,000 metal, 15,000 crystal and 5,000 hydrogen**.
- After a first full raid (90,000 taken), 50,000 metal, 30,000 crystal and 10,000 hydrogen remain: a second attack right after, with no production in between, can only take **45,000**.

## Common pitfalls

- **Bring cargo space**: combat ships carry little. Add Stellar Freighters to your attack fleet.
- **Losses shrink the cargo**: only surviving ships carry the loot.
- **A draw brings nothing**: you must wipe out the whole defense to plunder.
- **Switch targets**: each raid on the same player brings less than the previous one.
