# Defense rebuild

> **In short**: at the end of a battle, each destroyed defense has a **70% chance** of being rebuilt, for free and at once. The roll is made unit by unit: on average, 7 destroyed defenses out of 10 come back, but nothing is guaranteed. The Heritage dynasty and some talents raise this chance. Ships, on the other hand, are never rebuilt this way.
{.is-info}

## Rules

1. At the end of **every battle**, won, lost or drawn, **each destroyed defense** gets an **independent 70% roll**. On success it is rebuilt at no cost, at once; on failure it is lost.
2. It is **not a percentage applied to the batch**: with 10 defenses destroyed, between 0 and 10 come back, 7 on average.
3. The **Defensive Barrier** and the **Protective Dome** follow the same rule: a 70% chance of keeping them. Once lost, they can be built again at the shipyard.
4. Rebuilt defenses keep their points; only the defenses lost lower your score.
5. The rule also applies to a **moon**'s defenses, including during a [moon destruction](/en/combat/moon-destruction).
6. Defenses destroyed by **Long-Range Warheads** are **not** rebuilt this way: a missile strike destroys for good (unless a talent allows it).
7. **Ships** are never rebuilt for free. Only the [Repair Station](/en/economy/repair-station) takes part of them back, with no roll.
8. Stored missiles do not fight: they are not concerned.

### What changes the chance
9. The dynasty **The Heritage** raises the chance to **75%** (Nothing Is Lost trait).
10. Dynasty and class talents can raise it by **15 points** at most. Whatever the bonuses, it never goes above **85%**.
11. Some [Veteran](/en/dynasty/classes/veteran) talents always bring certain defenses back, or all of those of a second attack close behind; the [Admiral](/en/dynasty/classes/admiral)'s lower the chance for the defenders it attacks.

## Worked example

You lose 3 Ion Batteries in an attack:

| Batteries rebuilt | Probability at 70% | Probability at 75% (The Heritage) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 34.3% | 42.2% |
| 2 | 44.1% | 42.2% |
| 1 | 18.9% | 14.1% |
| 0 | 2.7% | 1.6% |

With 10 Ballistic Projectors destroyed, the chance of getting them all back is only **2.8%** (**5.6%** with The Heritage).

## Detailed data

Average number of defenses rebuilt:

| Defenses destroyed | At 70% | At 75% | At the ceiling (85%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.7 | 0.75 | 0.85 |
| 2 | 1.4 | 1.5 | 1.7 |
| 10 | 7 | 7.5 | 8.5 |
| 50 | 35 | 37.5 | 42.5 |
| 200 | 140 | 150 | 170 |

## Common pitfalls

- **Count on 70% on average, not for sure**: with few defenses, the gap can be large.
- **The Dome can vanish**: a 30% chance of losing it every time it is destroyed. You can then build a new one.
- **Enemy missiles destroy for good**: against Warheads, the rebuild does not apply.
- **Debris counts every defense destroyed**: in a universe where defenses leave debris, even those that come back leave some.
