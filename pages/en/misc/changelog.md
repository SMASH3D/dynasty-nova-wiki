# Wiki changelog

> **In short**: wiki changes, date by date.
{.is-info}

## October 2026

- **2 October 2026**: wiki created. Getting started pages, buildings, technologies, ships and defenses lists, and rules for economy, fleets, combat, espionage and alliances written. Values recorded on the Redline universe.
- **5 October 2026**: distance and flight time formulas reformatted.
- **6 October 2026**: flight time formula corrected (constant 35,000, speed factor from 1 to 10, following the game fix for trips that were ten times too short); 7-day inactivity on Redline; rotating shop and planet skin rarities; spying on players on vacation; reports your way; alliance announcement; private universe plan names and starting price.
- **6 October 2026 (continued)**: monthly planet temperature cycle detailed; attack alert 1 minute before impact.
- **6 October 2026 (dynasties)**: new [Dynasty and talents](/en/dynasty) section: dynasties, classes, talents, the common tree and the ten class trees. Movement, tutorial, defence rebuild and espionage updated accordingly.
- **8 October 2026**: talent tree diagram at the top of the detailed data of each [class](/en/dynasty/classes) and of the [common tree](/en/dynasty/common-tree).
- **15 October 2026**: Economy section checked against the game code. Automaton Factory level 5 cost corrected; buildings you can build on a moon; research one study at a time across the empire; how storage bonuses stack; Lunar base cannot be demolished.
- **15 October 2026 (alliances)**: Alliances pages checked against the game code. Alliance missions and station rewritten (convoys to the treasury, mutual aid, 3-tier station, missions by enrollment and relative effort); every member votes; rights by rank and function; renaming costs; fair play from 0 to 100; attack limit clarified.
- **15 October 2026 (appendix)**: new [appendix of buildings level by level](/en/economy/building-levels): cost, energy and output of every level, up to level 50.
- **15 October 2026 (bank)**: new [Stellar Bank](/en/economy/stellar-bank) page.
- **15 October 2026 (rewrite)**: every section rewritten from the game code, values and tables recomputed.
- **15 October 2026 (publications)**: [Economy formulas](/en/economy/formulas), [Orbital Dock](/en/fleet/orbital-dock) and [Moons](/en/universe/moons) pages published.
  - **Economy**: production, energy and storage tables level by level, full buildings catalogue, formulas page, how shipyard orders are paid, Repair Station after any battle in defense.
  - **Alliances**: ranks, functions and rights, recruitment, succession; pacts, fair play from 0 to 100, every member votes, wars and score.
  - **Research**: one study at a time, full duration formula, effects in figures, Graviton Technology energy, full tree, Stellar Collaboration network.
  - **Fleet**: drive switches, speed from 1 to 100%, wrap-around map, hydrogen in the holds, Stellar Station deposits, per-mission sending rules.
  - **Defense**: build times, defense queue and payment, missile range, flight, interception and damage.
  - **Combat**: plunder at 50% in every universe, explosions, 85% rebuild ceiling, moon sizes, simulator.
  - **Espionage**: spying on moons, shared alliance vision, intrusion alert, Sensor phalanx.
  - **Universe**: wrap-around map, Stellar Station, colonization, abandonment, position bonus in 9 to 20 position universes.
  - **Players**: military points for every ship, 7-day newcomer protection, report retention, frozen private universes, report settings.
  - **Dynasty and talents**: values fixed, class ultimates need no level, edge and lock rules, talent texts taken from the game.
