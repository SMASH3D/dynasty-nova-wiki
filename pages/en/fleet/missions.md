# Missions

> **In short**: a fleet always leaves on a mission. There are eight: Attack, Transport, Stationing, Colonization, Espionage, Recycling, Expedition and Moon destruction. Each has its own target and rules.
{.is-info}

> Ship names are provisional English translations: the game still shows French names (see the [glossary](/en/getting-started/glossary)).
{.is-warning}

## Rules

1. Each fleet takes a **fleet slot** until it returns.
2. Hydrogen for the **return is paid at departure**, except for Colonization and Stationing, which are one-way.
3. A fleet can be **recalled** at any time before the end of its outbound trip, with no hydrogen refund.
4. **There is no grouped attack and no allied defense**: you cannot station a fleet at an ally's planet.
5. The attacked player gets an **alert 1 minute before the impact** of an Attack.

## Detailed data

| Mission | Target | What happens | Good to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Attack** | Another player's planet or moon | Battle against fleet and defenses; on victory, 50% of each resource is plundered | Counts towards the [attack limit](/en/players/attack-limit); subject to [protections](/en/players/beginner-protection); ends your new player protection |
| **Transport** | Your planet or another player's, allies included | The cargo is delivered, the fleet returns | Not possible towards a player on vacation |
| **Stationing** | One of **your** planets or moons | The fleet and its cargo stay there | One-way; only to your own bases |
| **Colonization** | Free position | The Space Pioneer founds a colony and is consumed; the resources carried stay on the colony | One-way; needs a free colony slot (see [Colonization](/en/universe/colonization)) |
| **Espionage** | Another player's planet or moon | Scouts send back a report | Possible even on a protected player; see [Spying](/en/espionage/spying) |
| **Recycling** | Debris field | Salvagers collect debris, within their cargo | See [Debris fields](/en/combat/debris) |
| **Expedition** | The system's deep space | The fleet holds position, then returns with a find, or not | See [Expeditions](/en/fleet/expeditions) |
| **Moon destruction** | Another player's moon | Battle, then a destruction attempt | Stellar Colossus-only fleet; see [Moon destruction](/en/combat/moon-destruction) |

## Worked example

You send 5 Stellar Freighters to deliver resources to an ally and come back: hydrogen for the outbound **and** return trips is taken at departure. If you send the same Freighters on **Stationing** to your colony, only the outbound trip is charged and the fleet stays there.

## Common pitfalls

- **Stationing is not transport**: Stationing only targets your own bases. To supply an ally, use Transport.
- **An attack costs your protection**: the first attack you launch ends your 7 days of new player protection. Espionage and transport do not remove it.
- **Slots are limited**: every mission, expeditions included, takes a slot until the fleet returns.
